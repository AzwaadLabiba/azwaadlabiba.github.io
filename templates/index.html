Hi, I'm Azwaad Labiba Mohiuddin

My research focuses on low-light image enhancement with deep learning and Transformer architectures, with applications in medical imaging, autonomous vehicles and surveillance. I'm exploring how attention and classical image-processing ideas can combine for better results at lower compute. Before my PhD I spent two years as a software engineer, so I care about research that actually ships.

Powered by cold brew on long days and lattes during late-night idea storms.
